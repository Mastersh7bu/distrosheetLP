Privacy Policy for DistroSheet

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Who We Are

DistroSheet is a Google Sheets distribution and update tool owned and operated by Acumet Ltd. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact Dan at dan@acumetltd.com.

2. What DistroSheet Does

DistroSheet is a Chrome extension and web service that lets spreadsheet creators sign in with Google, distribute cloned copies of their Google Sheets to recipients, and push live formula or layout updates to those copies without overwriting the recipient's own data.

3. Information We Collect and Store

To make the extension work, we collect and store only the minimum data required:

  • Google Account Email: Used to identify your DistroSheet account.
  • OAuth Refresh Tokens: Stored securely on our server so DistroSheet can access Google Drive and Google Sheets on your behalf to clone files and push updates.
  • Sheet Configuration Data: Google Sheet file IDs and your tab settings (including protected cell ranges and skipped tabs).
  • Recipient Emails: The email addresses you enter when sending a copy of a sheet to a buyer or recipient.
  • Usage Logs: Basic event logs (such as publish and update timestamps) used to confirm successful deliveries and troubleshoot errors.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

We do not store copies of your actual spreadsheet files or your recipients' sheet data on our servers. All spreadsheets remain hosted exclusively within Google Drive—DistroSheet simply acts as the bridge to handle delivery and push updates.

Only the minimal account and configuration metadata needed to run the service is processed using the following infrastructure:

  • API & Hosting: Vercel
  • Database: Supabase (stores account info, OAuth tokens, sheet IDs, and protected range settings)
  • Spreadsheets & Permissions: Google’s systems (Google Drive and Google Sheets)

5. What We Don’t Do

Access is only used to send the sheet. That is how the Google permission works. We have nothing to do with the file data.

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or your recipients' email addresses.
  • We do not use your Google Sheets contents for advertising, marketing, or profiling.
  • We do not access your Google Drive files other than the specific sheets you register and distribute through DistroSheet.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

DistroSheet’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only use Google user data to provide and maintain DistroSheet’s core sheet distribution and syncing features.

7. Your Controls & Deleting Your Data

You stay in full control of your data at all times:

  • Revoke Google Access: You can disconnect DistroSheet at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.
  • Uninstall: You can remove the Chrome extension from your browser at any time.
  • Delete Account Data: Email dan@acumetltd.com to request permanent deletion of your account, stored OAuth tokens, sheet settings, and logs.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as DistroSheet evolves. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
